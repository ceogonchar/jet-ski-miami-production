Every rider must complete a rental agreement and liability waiver before boarding. The documents are signed in person at the marina during check-in.

All riders must follow staff instructions, the safety briefing, and applicable Florida boating laws. A valid photo ID and any required Florida boating safety certificate must be presented before the ride.

Signing a waiver does not replace the safety requirements or permit unsafe operation. Jetski Miami 7 may refuse or stop a ride when safety requirements are not met.
