Jet Ski Rental in North Bay Village & North Miami Beach

Short answer: Jet Ski Miami 7 rents self-guided jet skis on Biscayne Bay in the Miami Beach / North Bay Village area, the quiet northern side of the bay between the 79th Street Causeway and Haulover. If you are staying in Bal Harbour, Surfside, Sunny Isles Beach, Normandy Isles, North Beach or Aventura, we are the closest launch point: 10–20 minutes by car instead of a trip to South Beach or Downtown. Prices start at $189 per hour, everything included, with a $30 deposit to book.

Why launch from North Bay Village instead of South Beach

  • You start in the open bay. North of the 79th Street Causeway the bay is wide and residential, so you reach full-speed water within minutes instead of idling through a busy marina.
  • The sandbar is close. The Haulover sandbar is 5–10 minutes away by jet ski; a 2-hour rental covers it with a long swim stop.
  • The skyline is still reachable. Downtown Miami and Star Island are 20–25 minutes south, so a 3-hour ride does both.
  • Fewer crowds, easier logistics. Parking instructions come with your booking, and check-in takes minutes.

What the ride looks like from here

1 hour ($189): a loop through the Venetian Islands or a fast run north toward Haulover and back. Enough for a first ride.

2 hours ($349, 2.5 hours on the water): the sandbar swim stop, then back along the Miami Beach side with the Bal Harbour towers on your left.

3 hours ($499): sandbar plus the Downtown skyline and Star Island.

Full Day ($649, 4 hours): everything above with long stops, plus Key Biscayne and the Cape Florida lighthouse on calm days.

All rides are self-guided: safety briefing, recommended route, GPS speed zones that slow the jet ski automatically in no-wake areas. No instructor rides behind you.

Requirements and what to bring

  • The person who books and signs must be 21+ with photo ID. Drivers 14+ with a Florida boater safety card if born on or after January 1, 1988 (30-minute online certificate).
  • A credit card for the $150 refundable security hold per jet ski.
  • Sunscreen, towel, water, sunglasses with a strap. We provide life vests, fuel and GPS.
  • Arrive 30 minutes early. No alcohol before or during the ride.

Staying nearby? Plan the day

Ride in the morning when the bay is calmest, then lunch in Normandy Isles or on the 71st Street strip, afternoon at Haulover Park or Bal Harbour Shops, sunset on North Beach. For visitors with one day in the city, see what to do in Miami for a day.

Frequently asked questions

Where exactly do we meet for the rental?

On Biscayne Bay in the Miami Beach / North Bay Village area . Pickup and drop-off are at Bill Bird Marina in Haulover Park (10800 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33154); parking instructions are sent right after booking.

How far is it from Bal Harbour or Surfside?

About 10 minutes by car. From Sunny Isles Beach and Aventura about 20 minutes, from South Beach about 15 minutes.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking instructions come with your booking confirmation. Ask us before you arrive if you are coming in an oversized vehicle.

What can I see on a ride from North Bay Village?

The Haulover sandbar and inlet to the north, the Venetian Islands and Star Island to the south, the Downtown Miami skyline, Raccoon Island and Indian Creek.

Yes, especially for weekends. Online booking shows live availability; a $30 deposit holds 1–3 jet skis.

Can I rent for just one hour?

Yes. One hour is $189 per jet ski and is enough for the Venetian Islands loop or a run toward the sandbar.
