What is included in the price

  • Fuel for the whole rental. You never refuel or pay a fuel fee afterwards.
  • Life vests for every rider, in all sizes.
  • Safety briefing before launch: controls, no-wake zones, the route, how to get back.
  • GPS speed control: the jet ski automatically slows down inside no-wake zones, so you cannot get a ticket for speeding there.

What is not included

  • Second rider: $30 per jet ski.
  • Florida sales tax and booking/payment fees: calculated at checkout and shown before payment.

Deposit and payment explained

  1. $30 booking deposit reserves your date and time for 1–3 jet skis. For 4 or more jet skis, or a Full Day, the deposit is 20% of the total. The deposit counts toward your rental price.

The booking deposit is non-refundable, but if you cancel 48 hours or more before the start we move your ride once to any date within 90 days. If we cancel for weather or safety, unused time is refunded in full, including the deposit.

How to compare jet ski prices in Miami (the honest checklist)

When a quote looks cheaper, ask these five questions before you pay:

  1. Is fuel included? Fuel surcharges of $50–75 per hour are a common surprise at check-in.
  2. Is there a per-passenger fee? Some operators charge much more than $30 for a second rider.
  3. Is there a marina, dock or "equipment" fee? These are usually not in the advertised price.
  4. How big is the riding zone? A $100 "hour" inside a small roped-off area is a different product from 25 square miles of Biscayne Bay with a sandbar stop.
  5. How much time is actually on the water? A 60-minute slot that includes briefing and paperwork is shorter than it sounds. Our briefing happens before your clock starts, and the 2-hour package adds 30 minutes.

If the answer to all five is clear and in writing, the price is real. If not, expect the dock price to be higher than the ad.
