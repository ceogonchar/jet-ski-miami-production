Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to rent a jet ski in Miami?

One jet ski with one rider: 1 hour $189, 2 hours $349 (with a 30-minute bonus, so 2.5 hours on the water), 3 hours $499, Full Day (4 hours) $649. Fuel, life vests, GPS and the safety briefing are included. An extra passenger is $30. Tax and booking fees are shown at checkout before you pay.

Do I need a license to rent a jet ski in Florida?

No driver's license is needed. Drivers born on or after January 1, 1988 must hold a Florida boater safety card; a temporary certificate takes about 30 minutes online and we send you the link right after booking.

What is the minimum age?

The person who books and signs the rental agreement must be 21+ with a valid photo ID. The driver can be 14+ with a Florida boater safety card. Riders under 18 need a parent or guardian signature.

Can two people ride one jet ski?

Yes, up to 2 people can share a jet ski. The base price includes one rider; an extra passenger is $30.

Are rides self-guided?

Yes, every rental is self-guided. After a safety briefing you ride your own route (Haulover sandbar, Venetian Islands, Downtown skyline) inside a GPS-monitored zone. No instructor rides behind you.

Where are you located?

Pickup and drop-off are at Bill Bird Marina in Haulover Park, 10800 Collins Ave, Miami Beach, FL 33154 — on the bay side of Haulover Park, 5 minutes from Bal Harbour, 10 from Sunny Isles, 20 from South Beach, 25–30 from Downtown and PortMiami. Parking instructions are sent right after booking.

What is the booking deposit?

A $30 booking deposit reserves your slot for 1–3 jet skis (20% of the total for 4 or more jet skis or a Full Day). The balance is paid at check-in. A separate $150 refundable security hold is placed on a card at check-in and released about 5 business days after return.

What is your cancellation policy?

The booking deposit is non-refundable, but if you cancel 48 hours or more before the start we move your ride once to another date within 90 days. If we cancel for weather or safety, unused time is refunded in full.

Can beginners ride?

Absolutely. Most of our guests are first-timers; the briefing covers starting, stopping, no-wake zones and the route.

Do you offer bachelor or bachelorette party packages?

Yes. We provide up to 6 jet skis per booking for groups; the group rate is shown at checkout.

What are your hours?

Daily 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, 7 days a week, daylight rides only.
