Your booking deposit is what confirms the reservation. It is the price of taking a specific watercraft off sale for your time slot: from the moment it is paid we stop offering that unit for those hours and turn away other bookings for it. If you ride, the whole deposit is credited against the price of your rental, so it costs you nothing beyond the price you were quoted.

The deposit is not refundable. What your notice changes is whether the booking can be moved. Cancel 48 hours or more before your scheduled start and we move the deposit once to another date within 90 days, subject to availability; tell us in writing, by email or message, before that point. Cancel less than 48 hours before your scheduled start, or fail to arrive, and the deposit is retained and there is no reschedule, because at that notice the slot can no longer be resold.

The most we keep for a cancellation is the deposit you actually paid. The rest of the price is never owed, and any amount paid in advance above the deposit is refunded to the original payment method.

There is no refund for time you choose not to use. The price is for the block of time you reserved, not for the minutes you spend on the water. If you book three hours and come back after two, arrive late, or decide to finish early, the price does not change, because the watercraft was reserved for you and unavailable to anyone else for the whole block.

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before your start time for check-in and the safety briefing. Time lost to a late arrival comes out of your slot and does not extend it. Arriving without valid photo identification, without a Florida Boating Safety ID card or temporary certificate if you were born on or after January 1, 1988, or under the influence of drugs or alcohol, is treated the same as cancelling on the day: the deposit is retained and the balance is refunded.

If Jetski Miami 7 cancels, or ends a ride early, because of weather, water conditions, mechanical trouble or safety, the choice is yours: the unused time is refunded in full, including the deposit, or it is rescheduled at no charge.

Your right to dispute a charge with your bank or payment provider is not affected by anything above.
