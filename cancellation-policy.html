For a full refund, cancellations must be made at least 48 hours before the scheduled reservation time.

Cancellations made within 48 hours of the scheduled reservation and no-shows are non-refundable. A missed time slot caused by late arrival or missing required documentation is considered a no-show.

If Jetski Miami 7 cancels or cuts a ride short because of weather or safety conditions, unused time will be refunded or the reservation may be rescheduled free of charge.

Please arrive at least 30 minutes before your scheduled reservation for check-in and the safety briefing.
